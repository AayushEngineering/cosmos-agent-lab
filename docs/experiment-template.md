# Experiment record

## Question
What precise question is this experiment answering?

## Background and sources
List primary sources, datasets, versions, publication dates, and relevant limitations.

## Hypothesis
State a claim that could be wrong.

## Falsification condition
What observation would count against the hypothesis?

## Method
Describe data, tools, parameters, environment, and exact steps so another person can reproduce the work.

## Baseline
What simpler method or prior result is the comparison point?

## Results
Report positive, negative, and ambiguous results. Include uncertainty and deviations from plan.

## Limitations
List confounders, missing data, possible sources of bias, and assumptions.

## Reproducibility
Record code commit, environment, random seeds, data identifiers, and artifact locations.

## Review
Who independently checked the result? What changed after review?
